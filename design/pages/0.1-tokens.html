Type

Three families, each with one job. The webapp uses three heading sizes and keeps them on phones too, so titles never jump between breakpoints.

Aa--font-display · Sora

Page, section and card titles. 700, slightly tight.

Aa--font-body · Geist

Names, body, buttons, links, field labels, chat, game names, credential lines.

00--font-mono · Geist Mono

Money, dates, times, countdowns, statuses, ranks, ratings, counts, labels, @handles.
