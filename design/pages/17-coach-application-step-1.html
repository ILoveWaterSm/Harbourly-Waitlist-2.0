Coach on Harbourly

Coach gamers in one-hour sessions held in Harbourly. Here's how it works and what you'll need, so nothing comes as a surprise.

How it works

  1. ApplyTell us which games you coach and show us your rank. Our team reviews every application and emails you the outcome.
  2. Set up payoutsStripe, our payment partner, checks your identity and sets up where your payouts go.
  3. Set up your profileA photo, a short bio, the languages you coach in, your weekly hours and a price for each game.
  4. Go liveGamers can find you and book you. Your profile shows New Coach until you complete 5 sessions with no upheld problems.

What you'll need

  • A government IDStripe checks it when you set up payouts. Harbourly never sees or stores it.
  • A bank account in Singapore, in your nameThis is where your payouts go.
  • Screenshots of your rankOur team checks the rank you claim against them. Only the team sees them.

Money and sessions

  • Sessions are 60 minutes, 1-on-1, held in Harbourly: call, chat and screen share.
  • You set a price for each game, from S$10 a session. Gamers pay a 6% platform fee on top, which doesn't come out of your price.
  • Harbourly keeps 12% of your price. For a S$25.00 session you're paid S$22.00.
  • Each session is paid out automatically 72 hours after it ends, unless a problem is reported.
  • If you cancel inside 24 hours, or don't show up, the gamer gets a full refund and it counts against you.

You'll coach from Singapore, paid in SGD through Stripe. Coaching is open in Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines.
