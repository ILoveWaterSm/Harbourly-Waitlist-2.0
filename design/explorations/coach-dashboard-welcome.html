How your first session works

  1. A gamer books you

    You get an email with the game, date and time. Harbourly holds the gamer's payment until the session is confirmed.

  2. You meet in Harbourly

    Join session opens 10 minutes before the start, from Coach sessions. Talk, chat and share your screen.

  3. The session is confirmed

    The gamer confirms it went ahead, or it's confirmed automatically 72 hours after the end.

  4. You're paid

    Your price less the 12% commission goes to your payout account: ₱1,250.00 − ₱150.00 = ₱1,100.00.
