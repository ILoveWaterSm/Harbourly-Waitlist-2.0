The 20 minutes

StartsChapter, and the one thing they should take awayLength

About 15 minutes of demo, with 5 for the welcome, the wrap-up and questions. The walkthrough's clock turns amber if you're more than about a minute behind.

Before the meeting

  • Rehearse once with notes on (Rehearse with notes on, on the walkthrough page). It takes about 15 minutes and you'll know every click.
  • Open the walkthrough in the window you'll share, at least 1280 pixels wide, browser zoom at 100%. Close other tabs and mute notifications.
  • Open this guide on your phone or a second screen. Keep the notes in the walkthrough off while you're sharing.
  • Press Start presenting just before you begin. It resets the demo (logged out, nothing booked) and starts the clock on the welcome slide.
  • Know your three questions for the end. They're on the wrap-up slide, but say them in your own words.
  • Have a clicker? It works. Most send Page Down and Page Up, the same as → and ←.

Keeping it casual

  • Tell it as a story. "I'm a gamer, I want to get better at Mobile Legends, let's find a coach." Then every screen is just the next thing you'd do.
  • One idea per screen. Each screen has a few talking points. Pick the one or two that matter for this audience and move on. You don't have to say all of them.
  • Don't read the screen. They can see it. Say why it's there: "This is so you never pay without knowing the refund rule."
  • Say it's a design up front. "It's clickable, but nothing's live, so no real payments or emails." Then nobody worries when a dashed demo shortcut appears.
  • Pause twice: after checkout ("Would you feel OK paying like this?") and after the dispute ("Does that feel fair?"). Those reactions are the most useful thing you'll get.
  • Park the deep questions. "Good one, let me come back to that at the end." Then make sure you do.
  • When a step loads by itself (Logging in, Confirming your payment, Kairo joining), keep talking. It moves on within a few seconds.

Talk track

The time is when that screen should come up. "Then" is what Next clicks for you (the walkthrough rings it in green).

Running long or short

Running long

  • Press S to skip to the next chapter.
  • On My sessions, skip cancelling and the new-time request: press S on the dashboard to go straight to the session (saves about 2 minutes).
  • In the session, skip the camera and banners. Just end it.
  • Cut the dispute chapter to one sentence: "If there's a problem, the money waits and the team decides from both sides."

Got spare time

  • Phones: Demo panel (from Notes, or press H to hide the bar and use the Demo button) → Width → Phone.
  • Log in: Demo panel → 14 Log in, then Log in. Updated terms: Demo panel → 16 Accept updated terms.
  • Forgot password: 14 Log in → Forgot password? → the reset email → set a new password.
  • Edge cases: Demo panel → 03 Pick a slot → "Slot just taken", or 13 → "Under 18 · blocked".

If they ask

How does Harbourly make money?

Gamers pay the coach's price plus a 6% platform fee. Coaches pay a 12% commission, taken from their payout. For Kairo: ₱1,850 + ₱111 fee = ₱1,961 paid, and Kairo receives ₱1,628.

When does the coach get paid?

When the gamer confirms the session went ahead, or automatically 72 hours after it ends. Reporting a problem pauses that until the team decides.

What if I need to cancel?

Up to 1 hour before the start: a full refund, fee included. Inside the last hour: half the coach's price comes back, and the coach is paid the other half. If the coach cancels, the gamer always gets a full refund.

What if the coach doesn't show up?

After 15 minutes the gamer can report a no-show. The coach has 24 hours to respond. With no response, the gamer is refunded in full automatically. Otherwise the team checks the call's join records. Repeat no-shows get a warning, then suspension.

And if the gamer doesn't show up?

The coach can report it. If the team agrees, the coach's price is split 50/50, the same as a late cancellation.

Can I move a session?

Yes, until 24 hours before. You offer up to 3 of the coach's free times, and they pick one. An unanswered request expires after 48 hours, or 12 hours before the original start. A booking can be moved once.

How are coaches checked?

Every coach goes through identity and payout checks: Stripe or Xendit check a government ID and a bank account in their country before they can go live. They also show proof of rank. New coaches carry a New Coach label until they've built a track record. The coach application is the Phase 5 work.

Which countries and currencies?

Gamers in 10 Southeast Asian countries at launch. Coaches in Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines first, where payouts work. You see prices in your own currency; you're charged in the coach's, and your bank converts.

How long is a session, and what does it cost?

Every session is one hour, 1-on-1. Coaches set their own price per game, with a floor of about S$10.

Is this the real app?

No, it's the design: every screen and state built as clickable pages. The app is built from these next. Payments will use Stripe and Xendit; sign-in uses Supabase, with emails through Resend and phone codes through Twilio.

Does it work on phones?

Every screen is designed for phones too. Show it: Demo panel → Width → Phone. Screen sharing in a session needs a computer; on a phone you can talk, chat and watch.

What's next?

Phase 5, the coach side: applying to coach, payout and profile setup, and the coach dashboard. After that, account settings and the coach's profile settings.

If something breaks

  • Clicked the wrong thing? The bar says "Off the route". Press Next and you're back on track.
  • A screen looks stuck? Refresh the page. The walkthrough keeps your place and your clock.
  • Lost completely? Press N, then End walkthrough, then Start presenting again. Use S to skip to the chapter you were on.
  • No internet at the venue? Run it from the repo instead: python3 -m http.server at the repo root, then open /design/demo/walkthrough.html.
