Run sheet

The clock in the bar turns amber if you're more than about a minute behind. Press S to skip ahead a chapter.

Controls

  • →PgDnNext: clicks the highlighted control, or moves on
  • ←PgUpBack one step
  • NShow or hide your notes (keep them off while sharing)
  • SSkip to the next chapter
  • HHide or show the bar

A presentation clicker works too: most send Page Down and Page Up. You can also click anything yourself. If you wander off the route, Next brings you back.
