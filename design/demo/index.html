Harbourly webapp demo

Use the gamer side of Harbourly as one app: find a coach, book and pay, hold the session, confirm and review, then sign up, log in and get back into your account. Pick a journey to start.

The Demo button

Bottom left on every screen. Use it to jump to any screen and state, including errors, loading and edge cases, to preview at phone width, or to show clickable areas.

Dates jump between screens

Each screen keeps the date and time it was designed at (Thu 24 Sep, Mon 5 Oct, Thu 8 Oct), so the clock moves as you click through.

Gamer side only

The coach side (Phase 5 onwards) isn't included. Become a coach and the coach choice when setting up show a short note instead. Dashed boxes are demo shortcuts, such as opening the link in an email.
