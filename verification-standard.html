01
First layer of verification

Your identity. Your credentials.

Completed once, before a coach can go live. This is how we confirm you are who you say you are.

Your identity is confirmed through Stripe or Xendit, the same payment providers that handle your payouts. When you set up how you get paid, they run a real identity check backed by a government issued ID, the same standard used by regulated financial platforms. Harbourly never sees or stores your ID or banking details directly.

  • Identity confirmed through Stripe or Xendit as part of payout setup
  • Real government ID backed check, run by a regulated payment provider, not by Harbourly
  • Coaches must be 18 or over
  • A coach cannot appear as bookable until this check is complete

Clear this check and your profile earns the Verified badge — the same one shown on every coach's profile.

02
First layer of verification

Optional. Your track record

Not required to earn your badge, but it's what turns a verified profile into a proven one.

Have tournament results or a marketplace track record? Submit it for our team to review by hand. It's optional and won't block your Verified badge — confirmed evidence just gets added to your profile under a separate Credentials reviewed label.

03
Second layer of verification

We watch. Continuously.

Earning the badge is not the end of the process. Every coach's standing on Harbourly is reviewed on an ongoing basis, not just at signup.

Reviews and ratings

Every session ends with a rating from the student. Our team monitors reviews for authenticity and looks at patterns over time, not just individual scores, so what you see on a coach's profile reflects real sessions.

Dispute history

If a session doesn't go as expected, either party can raise it with us directly. Every dispute is reviewed by our team by hand. A coach's dispute history is taken into account when we assess their ongoing standing on the platform. This protects students while making sure coaches are not judged unfairly on a single report.

Delivery

Students rate their sessions honestly, and that feedback shapes how we view a coach's reliability over time. A coach whose sessions consistently fall short of what their profile promises will be reviewed by our team.

Payment protection

Your money never leaves without your permission.

Every session on Harbourly is protected by escrow, held securely by our payment provider, for players and coaches alike.

01

Player books and pays

When you pay at checkout, your payment is captured and held securely, not sent to the coach yet.

02

Session is completed

After your session, you have 72 hours to confirm it went well. If you don't take any action, it confirms automatically after 72 hours, so a coach is never left waiting indefinitely. If something went wrong, you can report it instead, which pauses everything while we review.

03

Funds released to coach

Once you confirm, or once the 72 hour window passes with no issue raised, payment is released to your coach. If a dispute is raised, funds stay held until we've reviewed it and reached a fair outcome.
