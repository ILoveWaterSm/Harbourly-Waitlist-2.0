Part of getting approved

Legal identity

Identity is confirmed through Stripe or Xendit, the same payment providers that handle payouts. When a coach sets up how they get paid, the provider runs a real identity check backed by a government-issued ID, the same standard used by regulated financial platforms. Harbourly never sees or stores the ID or banking details directly.

Once a coach clears the check above, there are two ways to reach the Verified badge: show a portfolio our team can confirm, or build a clean track record over the first 5 sessions as a New Coach. Both lead to the same badge.

Path A — Portfolio check

Show us proof from your history — tournament results, ratings, past coaching — and if it holds up, the badge is earned immediately.

Path B — New Coach

No portfolio needed. Start taking bookings right away and earn the badge after 5 clean sessions.

04
No portfolio, or it didn't hold up

No portfolio? Start as a New Coach.

Identity verification is already done, so a coach can start taking bookings right away.

If there's no portfolio to submit, or it doesn't hold up in review, a coach can still start taking bookings right away as a New Coach, since identity verification is already complete. They'll show as a New Coach, visible on their profile, until they complete 5 sessions with no serious disputes. Clear that, and the same Verified badge is earned, no different from the portfolio path.

If a serious dispute comes up during this period, our team reviews it directly. Depending on what happened, the coach may continue toward their 5 sessions, have their progress reset, or be suspended. Nothing here is automatic. Every case gets looked at.

05
Optional, once you're set up

Want gamers to hear you first?

A short voice introduction on your profile — entirely optional, and separate from earning your badge.

Coaches can add a short voice introduction to their profile — a chance for gamers to hear you before they book. It's entirely optional and has nothing to do with earning your badge or being able to take bookings.

Before any recording goes live, our team listens to it. We're checking for anything vulgar, inappropriate, or otherwise not okay for a public profile, nothing more. Once it clears, it's added to your profile for gamers to hear.

06
Beyond the badge

The checks above confirm who a coach is. We're also rolling out formal background checks as an additional trust layer on top of that, not required to earn the badge. This doesn't hold up any coach from listing today.

07
After the badge

We keep watching.

Earning the badge isn't the end of the review — every coach's standing is reviewed on an ongoing basis.

Earning the badge isn't the end of the review. Every coach's standing gets reviewed on an ongoing basis, not just once at signup, whether they're a New Coach or already Verified.

Reviews and ratings

Every session ends with a rating from the student. We monitor reviews for authenticity and watch for patterns over time, not just single scores, so what's on a coach's profile reflects real sessions.

Dispute history

If a session doesn't go as expected, either side can raise it with us directly. Every dispute gets reviewed by our team by hand within 72 hours. A coach's dispute history factors into how we assess their ongoing standing. This protects students without punishing a coach for one bad report.

Delivery

Student feedback shapes how we view a coach's reliability over time. A coach whose sessions consistently fall short of what their profile promises gets reviewed by our team.

Payment protection

Your money doesn't move without your permission.

Every session on Harbourly is protected by escrow, held by our payment provider, for players and coaches alike.

01

Book and pay

Payment is captured and held at checkout. It isn't sent to the coach yet.

02

Session happens

After the session, you have 72 hours to confirm it went well. No action needed, it confirms automatically once the window closes, so a coach is never left waiting indefinitely. If something went wrong, report it instead, and everything pauses while we review.

03

Funds release

Once you confirm, or the 72-hour window passes with nothing raised, payment releases to the coach. If a dispute is raised, funds stay held until we've reviewed it, within 72 hours, and reached a fair outcome.
